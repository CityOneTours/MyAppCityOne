Day by Day

Your Itinerary

5 unforgettable days across Dubai & Abu Dhabi

DAY1
🛬 Dubai Arrival

Dubai Airport Arrival & Marina Dhow Cruise

Arrive at Dubai Airport and transfer to Majestic Premier / City Retreat Hotel Bur Dubai for check-in. Spend a magical evening gliding along the illuminated Dubai Marina on a traditional Dhow Cruise, complete with a lavish dinner and live entertainment shows.

Airport Transfer Hotel Check-In Marina Dhow Cruise Dinner Included
DAY2
🏙️ City & Heights

Dubai City Tour + Burj Khalifa At the Top

Explore Dubai's iconic landmarks on a guided half-day city tour. Then soar to the top of the world at Burj Khalifa — visit the breathtaking 124th & 125th observation floors during exclusive non-peak hours (0800–1130 hrs or 2030–2300 hrs).

Half-Day City Tour Burj Khalifa 124F & 125F Non-Peak Hours
DAY3
🏜️ Desert Adventure

Afternoon Red Dunes Desert Safari

Buckle up for an exhilarating Red Dunes Desert Safari in powerful 4×4 vehicles. Experience the thrill of dune bashing, then settle in at a traditional desert camp for a sumptuous BBQ dinner under a canopy of stars with belly dance, tanoura & fire shows.

4×4 Dune Bashing BBQ Dinner Live Entertainment Standard Camp
DAY4
🎢 Yas Island

Transfer to Yas Island + Free Theme Park

Check out from your Dubai hotel and transfer to the thrilling Yas Island, Abu Dhabi. Check in to the stunning Radisson Blu or Crowne Plaza Yas Island hotel and enjoy entry to 1 complimentary world-class theme park of your choice — Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld, or Warner Bros. World!

Hotel Transfer Radisson Blu / Crowne Plaza 1 Free Theme Park
DAY5
🕌 Abu Dhabi & Departure

Abu Dhabi City Tour → Grand Mosque, BAPS Temple & Airport Drop

Check out and embark on a captivating Abu Dhabi City Tour. Marvel at the grandeur of the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque — one of the world's most magnificent mosques — and visit the stunning BAPS Hindu Mandir. Conclude your journey with a comfortable transfer to Dubai Airport for departure.

Grand Mosque BAPS Temple Abu Dhabi Tour Airport Drop
