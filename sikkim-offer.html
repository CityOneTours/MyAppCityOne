Day by Day Plan

Your 9-Day Adventure

Every moment carefully curated so you experience the very best of the Eastern Himalayas.

01
✈️
Day 1
Ahmedabad → Bagdogra / Siliguri
Fly from Ahmedabad to Bagdogra. Meet your tour leader, board your vehicle and head to Gangtok — the capital of Sikkim. Check in and evening at leisure.
✈ Flight🚐 Transfer🏨 Hotel
02
🏯
Day 2
Gangtok Sightseeing
Visit Rumtek Monastery, Enchey Monastery, Namgyal Institute of Tibetology and the beautiful flower exhibition centre. Evening stroll on MG Marg.
🧘 Monastery🎫 Tickets
03
🏔️
Day 3
Tsomgo Lake & Nathula Pass
Journey to the sacred Tsomgo (Changu) Lake at 12,400 ft, then proceed to the historic Nathula Pass on the India-China border at 14,140 ft. Breathtaking landscapes.
🌊 Lake🇮🇳 Border❄ Snow
04
🌄
Day 4
Gangtok → Pelling
Scenic drive to Pelling. En route visit Ravangla and the massive statue of Buddha. Arrive Pelling with dramatic views of Kangchenjunga peak. Sunset at leisure.
🚐 Drive🏔 Mountain
05
🗼
Day 5
Pelling Sightseeing
Visit Pemayangtse Monastery, Rabdentse Ruins, Singshore Bridge (highest suspension bridge in India), and the stunning Kanchenjunga Waterfalls.
🧘 Monastery🌉 Bridge💧 Waterfall
06
🚂
Day 6
Pelling → Darjeeling
Travel to Darjeeling, the iconic "Queen of Hills". Check in, refresh, and enjoy an evening walk to the Mall Road. Taste Darjeeling's world-famous first flush tea.
☕ Tea🏨 Hotel
07
🌅
Day 7
Tiger Hill Sunrise & City Tour
Early morning drive to Tiger Hill for the legendary Kangchenjunga sunrise. Visit Ghum Monastery, Batasia Loop, and the iconic Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (Toy Train).
🌅 Sunrise🚞 Toy Train🎫 Tickets
08
🍃
Day 8
Tea Gardens & Rock Garden
Visit Darjeeling's lush tea gardens, the stunning Rock Garden and Ganga Maya Park. Evening at leisure for shopping on Nehru Road. Farewell dinner.
🌿 Tea Garden🛍 Shopping
09
🛫
Day 9
Darjeeling → Bagdogra → Ahmedabad
Morning transfer to Bagdogra Airport. Board flight back to Ahmedabad with memories to last a lifetime. Tour ends on arrival.
✈ Flight Home🏠 Ahmedabad
