Full Itinerary
Day-by-Day Journey
5 days of curated experiences across Cairo and Alexandria — history, culture, cuisine and spectacle.
Day 1 Arrival in Cairo 26 May ▾
  • Arrival at Cairo International Airport
  • Clear immigration and customs formalities
  • Warm welcome by our dedicated representative
  • Transfer to hotel with guaranteed early check-in
  • Free time to relax and recover from your journey
  • Overnight stay at the hotel in Cairo
Day 2 Cairo City Tour & Nile Dinner Cruise 27 May ▾
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Half-day Cairo city tour
  • Visit Tahrir Square, drive past the Cairo Citadel, and visit the Mosque of Mohamed Ali
  • Explore areas reflecting both modern and historic Cairo
  • Evening transfer for Nile Dinner Cruise aboard the Nile Crystal
  • Open buffet of international and Egyptian cuisine
  • Live entertainment: belly dancing, traditional folklore shows & Tanoura performances
  • Transfer back to hotel — overnight in Cairo
Day 3 Full-Day Tour to Alexandria 28 May ▾
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Full-day excursion to Alexandria — Egypt's beautiful Mediterranean city
  • Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa — one of the Seven Wonders of the Middle Ages
  • Historic Pompey's Pillar
  • Photo stop at the Citadel of Qaitbay — built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria
  • Visit the Bibliotheca Alexandrina (outside or inside, subject to time)
  • Return to Cairo — overnight stay
Day 4 Pyramids, Sphinx & Grand Egyptian Museum 29 May ▾
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Full-day guided tour of Cairo's most iconic landmarks
  • Giza Plateau — Great Pyramids of Cheops, Chephren & Mykerinos (Seven Wonders of the Ancient World)
  • Explore the Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple
  • Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) — Egypt's most extensive collection of ancient artifacts and Pharaonic treasures
  • Transfer back to hotel — overnight in Cairo
Day 5 Khan El Khalili & Old Cairo | Departure 30–31 May ▾
  • Breakfast at the hotel and check-out
  • Explore Old Cairo — historic churches and mosques (subject to time and accessibility)
  • Visit the famous Khan El Khalili Bazaar — one of the oldest and most vibrant markets in the Middle East
  • Free time for shopping: souvenirs, spices, perfumes, jewelry & handicrafts
  • Transfer to Cairo International Airport for departure
  • Departure flight CAI → DXB at 01:40 (31 May) — arrives Dubai 06:20
