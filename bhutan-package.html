Day by Day

7-Day Sacred
Journey

Perfectly paced — enough time at every destination, with no rush and nothing left out.

Day 01 — Arrival Paro
Day 02 — Paro Valley

01
Day 1 · Arrival
Paro International Airport
▼
Fly into Paro — one of the world's most dramatic airport landings between steep Himalayan cliffs. Transfer to hotel. Welcome dinner with your tour leader and orientation for the week ahead.
✈ Flight🏨 Hotel🍽 Welcome Dinner
02
Day 2 · Sightseeing
Paro Valley Exploration
▼
Explore Rinpung Dzong (15th-century fortress-monastery), the National Museum of Bhutan housing artifacts from 7 centuries, the ancient Kyichu Lhakhang temple, and Paro town bazaar for local crafts.
🏯 Rinpung Dzong🏛 Museum🛕 Kyichu Temple🎫 All Tickets
03
Day 3 · Highlight
Tiger's Nest Monastery Trek
▼
The undeniable highlight of the journey. Trek to the legendary Taktsang Monastery perched 900m above the Paro Valley on a sheer cliff face. One of the most sacred Buddhist sites on earth. Afternoon at leisure in Paro.
🥾 Trek ~4hrs🙏 Monastery📸 Cliff Views
04
Day 4 · Capital City
Thimphu & Cultural Show
▼
Drive to Thimphu (1.5hrs). Visit Tashichho Dzong, the 51m-tall Buddha Dordenma statue, Memorial Chorten, Folk Heritage Museum and the vibrant weekend crafts market. Evening: traditional Bhutanese cultural performance with mask dances.
🏯 Tashichho Dzong🗿 Buddha Statue🎭 Cultural Show🛍 Market
05
Day 5 · Mountain Drive
Dochula Pass → Punakha
▼
Scenic drive over Dochula Pass at 3,100m with 108 memorial chortens and a sweeping Himalayan panorama. Descend into the warm, lush Punakha valley. Visit the magnificent Punakha Dzong at the confluence of Pho Chhu and Mo Chhu rivers.
🏔 Dochula Pass🏯 Punakha Dzong🌊 River Confluence
06
Day 6 · Valley
Punakha Sightseeing
▼
Visit Chimi Lhakhang — the temple of the "Divine Madman" set in paddy fields. Cross Bhutan's longest suspension bridge over the Mo Chhu River. Enjoy a riverside picnic lunch. Return to Thimphu for farewell dinner.
🛕 Chimi Temple🌉 Suspension Bridge🍽 Farewell Dinner
07
Day 7 · Departure
Paro Airport → Home
▼
Transfer to Paro Airport for your return flight. Carry memories of Bhutan's golden monasteries, fluttering prayer flags, ancient dzongs and the warmth of the world's happiest kingdom. Tour ends on arrival at your home city.
✈ Flight Home🙏 Farewell
