About Vietnam

Where Ancient Culture
Meets Adventure

Vietnam stretches 1,650 km along the eastern edge of mainland Southeast Asia — a land of contrasts. Misty mountains in the north, lantern-lit ancient towns in the centre, and bustling cities in the south, all woven together by a cuisine that will change your life.

Vietnamese Cuisine

A Food Lover's Paradise

Vietnam's street food scene is legendary worldwide — here are the iconic dishes you must try

Phở
Vietnam's iconic rice noodle soup with fragrant broth, fresh herbs, and your choice of beef or chicken
Bánh Mì
The legendary Vietnamese baguette sandwich — crispy bread stuffed with pâté, pickled vegetables, and fresh herbs
Cà Phê Trứng
Hanoi's famous egg coffee — a rich, creamy blend of egg yolks and condensed milk over strong Vietnamese drip coffee
Gỏi Cuốn
Fresh Vietnamese spring rolls wrapped in delicate rice paper with shrimp, herbs, rice noodles, and hoisin sauce
