Important Notice

City One Tourism LLC is a private travel agency and is not affiliated with any government authority. We assist travelers with visa application processing and documentation support. Final approval or rejection of visas is solely at the discretion of the respective immigration authorities.

Visa Processing

We provide complete assistance with documentation review, application submission guidance, and status tracking. Processing timelines vary depending on the destination country.

Service Charges

Our service fees are clearly communicated before application submission. Government visa fees are separate and may vary based on nationality and visa category.

Refund Policy

Service charges are non-refundable once application processing has begun. Government visa fees are non-refundable under any circumstances after submission.
