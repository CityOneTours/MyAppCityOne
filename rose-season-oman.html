🌹 Why Jabal Akhdar

Where the Mountains Bloom with Roses

Every spring, the terraced mountain villages of Jabal Akhdar in Oman's Al Dakhiliyah region transform into a breathtaking sea of pink and red Damask roses. This centuries-old tradition sees local farmers harvesting the prized flowers before dawn, distilling them into the world-famous Omani rose water.

CityOne Tourism offers you an exclusive, intimate experience — walk through fragrant rose gardens, meet the farmers, and witness the ancient art of rose distillation, all high in the cool mountain air.
