Important Disclaimer

City One Tourism LLC is a private travel and tourism company. We provide assistance with visa application processing and travel documentation support. We are not affiliated with any government or immigration authority. All visa approvals are subject to the respective country's immigration rules and regulations.

Our Visa Assistance Service

City One Tourism assists travelers in applying for tourist visas by helping with documentation, application submission, and processing follow-up. Our experienced team ensures a smooth and hassle-free visa application process.

  • Visa application assistance
  • Document verification
  • Application submission support
  • Status updates
  • Travel consultation

Visa Processing Time

Visa processing time depends on the immigration authority of the respective country.

  • UAE Tourist Visa – 24 to 72 hours
  • Oman Tourist Visa – 2 to 4 working days
  • Other Countries – Depends on embassy approval

Processing times may vary depending on application details and immigration regulations.

Refund Policy

Visa processing fees are non-refundable once the application has been submitted to the immigration authority. Service charges paid for consultation and application assistance may also be non-refundable depending on the stage of processing.

If an application cannot be processed due to documentation issues before submission, partial refunds may apply.
